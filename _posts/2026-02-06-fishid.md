I’ve been building **FishID**, a small web app aimed at *new Ohio anglers* who want to get better at answering the most common question after a catch:

> “What fish is this?”

It’s part identification tool, part learning game, and (eventually) part field guide.

If you’re the type of person who likes both fishing *and* tinkering with side projects, FishID is basically my attempt to bring those together: a clean, mobile-first experience that feels at home on the bank of a pond or in the boat on Lake Erie.

Try it here:

- https://fishid.theoutdoorprogrammer.com/

## The problem
If you’re just getting into fishing in Ohio, you’ll run into a few challenges fast:

- A lot of species look *similar* (especially sunfish, shiners/minnows, and some catfish)
- You want to follow the rules (size limits, seasons, protected species), but you can’t do that if you don’t know what you caught
- Most “fish ID” resources are either a PDF you never open on the water, or a deep biology page that isn’t beginner-friendly

FishID is my attempt at a quick, friendly path from “I think it’s a…” to “I’m confident that’s a…”

- **Identify mode** – see a fish photo and pick the right species
- **Key feature questions** – learn the “one thing” that separates lookalikes
- **Habitat questions** – connect fish to where you’d realistically find them in Ohio
- **True/false** – quick fact checks to reinforce fundamentals
- **Spot-the-difference** – a harder mode for similar species

Under the hood, it’s a straightforward web app with a fish dataset that drives lessons and quiz prompts. The goal isn’t to be fancy—it’s to be *fast*, *clear*, and *useful on a phone*.

Devlog-ish note: it’s built as a lightweight web app (PWA-style) so it can feel snappy and be usable even when your signal is questionable.

## Ohio-first content (on purpose)
The target user is an Ohio beginner, so the content leans Ohio:

- Habitat options include things like **Lake Erie and tributaries**, **Ohio inland lakes and reservoirs**, **Ohio River and large tributaries**, and **Ohio creeks and small rivers**
- References lean toward **ODNR** resources where possible

Long-term, I want FishID to help answer practical questions like:

- Is this species common in the water I’m fishing?
- What are the most reliable visual ID cues?
- What’s the “don’t confuse this with…” list for the species?

## A quick story (why this exists)
If you’ve ever stood there with a fish in hand thinking “okay… what *are* you?”, you know the moment. You don’t want a 30-minute biology rabbit hole—you want 2–3 reliable ID cues and a quick sanity check that you’re not mixing it up with a similar species.

FishID is built for that moment.

## What’s next
Here’s what I’m planning to add next:

- **More Ohio-specific references per species** (ODNR pages where available)
- **Better “lookalike” pairings** (and clearer hints)
- **Rule reminders** (not as legal advice, but as “double check ODNR regs” prompts)
- **Regional lesson packs** (Lake Erie basics vs. smallmouth rivers vs. farm ponds)

## Why I’m building it
I like building tools that I’d actually use. Fishing is one of those hobbies where knowledge compounds quickly, but the first few months can be confusing.

FishID is meant to make the learning curve shorter—and to help new anglers make good calls in the moment.
