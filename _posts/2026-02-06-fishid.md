There’s a very specific feeling every new angler runs into.

You’re standing on the bank of an Ohio creek with wet hands and muddy boots, holding a fish that’s flopping like it’s trying to escape your life choices… and you’re thinking:

- “What *is* this?”
- “Is it legal to keep?”
- “Am I about to confidently mis-identify this thing and get roasted?”

I’ve been there. That moment is why I’ve been building **FishID** — a small side project meant to help Ohio anglers answer the oldest question in the book:

> “What did I just catch?”

It’s part field guide, part learning game, and built around the fish you’re actually likely to hook in Ohio.

Try it here:

- https://fishid.theoutdoorprogrammer.com/

## Why this exists
Ohio fishing is awesome, but the learning curve can be steep. We’ve got a bunch of species that look like cousins — especially once you get into sunfish, crappie, stocked hybrids like saugeye, and all the “wait… which catfish is that?” moments.

Most fish ID resources fall into one of two buckets:

1) A PDF you *swear* you’ll use… but never open on the water
2) A biology rabbit hole with fin-ray counts when you really just need two good visual clues

I wanted something that feels more like a buddy standing next to you saying: “Look at the mouth. Look at the tail. Now you know.”

The app focuses on the stuff that actually helps:

- **Visual ID practice**: quick quizzes to train your eye on shapes and patterns
- **Key features**: the 1–2 “tells” that separate lookalikes
- **Habitat context**: connecting fish to where you’d realistically find them in Ohio
- **Spot-the-difference**: a harder mode for the fish people confuse most

## Ohio-first (on purpose)
FishID is aimed at new Ohio anglers, so the content leans local:

- Habitats include things like **Lake Erie and tributaries**, **Ohio inland lakes and reservoirs**, **Ohio River and large tributaries**, and **Ohio creeks and small rivers**
- References lean toward **ODNR** resources where possible

Long-term, I want it to help with practical “on the water” confidence:

- “Is this species common in the water I’m fishing?”
- “What’s the fastest visual check to confirm?”
- “What’s the fish people mix this up with?”

## The tech (briefly)
For the tech-curious: FishID is a lightweight web app (PWA-style). I built it that way because I want it to feel fast on a phone, and still be usable when your signal is questionable.

No app store download needed — just open the link and go.

## What’s next
A few things I’m planning to add:

- **More Ohio-specific references per species** (ODNR pages where available)
- **Better lookalike pairings** (and clearer hints)
- **Gentle rule reminders** (not legal advice — more like “double check ODNR regs” prompts)
- **Regional lesson packs** (Lake Erie basics vs. smallmouth rivers vs. farm ponds)
