TheOutdoorProgrammer SMS Forwarding Service
Last updated: February 16, 2026

Program Name: TheOutdoorProgrammer SMS Forwarding Service

Description: This service receives SMS messages sent to a shared Twilio
phone number and forwards them to pre-configured personal phone numbers.
It is designed for household use, enabling multiple family members to
receive shared two-factor authentication codes and service notifications.

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies. You will receive forwarded
messages only when an SMS is sent to the shared Twilio number. No
recurring or scheduled messages are sent.

Message and Data Rates: Message and data rates may apply. Check with your
wireless carrier for details about your messaging plan.

Opt-Out: Text **STOP** to the Twilio number at any time to stop receiving
forwarded messages. You will receive a confirmation and no further
messages will be sent to your number.

Help: Text **HELP** to the Twilio number for support information. You may
also contact us at joey@theoutdoorprogrammer.com for assistance.
