Privacy Policy
TheOutdoorProgrammer SMS Service
Last updated: February 16, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the SMS forwarding service operated by
TheOutdoorProgrammer (theoutdoorprogrammer.com).

What We Collect
- Phone numbers you register with the service
- SMS message content received and forwarded by the service

How We Use It
- Solely to forward incoming SMS messages to your configured phone numbers
- We do not store message content beyond what is needed for delivery

What We Don't Do
- We do not sell, share, or distribute your data to third parties
- We do not use your phone number or message content for marketing
- We do not send promotional or advertising messages
- We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers

Data Retention
- Message content is not retained after forwarding
- Phone number configuration is stored only as needed to operate the service
